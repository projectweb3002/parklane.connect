PARKLANE GOOGLE SHEETS INTEGRATION

1. Replace the current Code.gs in your spreadsheet-bound Apps Script project with Parklane_Lead_Automation_Code.gs.
2. Save, then Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit > New version > Deploy.
3. Keep the existing /exec URL (it is already configured in config/config.js).
4. In Google Sheets, add these extra headers after Status (column M), starting at N1:
Purchase Timeline | Purchase Objective | Family Size | Preferred Facilities | Referrer | UTM Medium | UTM Content | UTM Term | Privacy Consent
5. Upload/deploy the entire parklane-landing-pages folder to your website, preserving its folder structure.
6. Before sending paid traffic, submit one real test lead from each page and confirm each appears in the Leads sheet.
7. The browser uses fetch(no-cors), so it cannot read the Apps Script response. A browser-side success/redirect is not proof of a row being saved; verify in the Sheet during testing.

SECURITY / OPERATIONS
- The Web App endpoint is public. Add server-side spam controls (e.g. honeypot + rate limiting / CAPTCHA) before scaling paid traffic.
- Do not put private API keys in frontend files.
- Ensure your privacy notice accurately explains collection and use of lead details.
