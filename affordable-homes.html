HOME-BUYING CHECKLIST

Five Essentials to Review Before You Buy

Smart home buying is about looking beyond the headline price to the complete cost and everyday livability of the property.

Total All-In Budget

Factor in stamp duty, registration, parking, maintenance deposits, and interior essentials alongside the base price.

Carpet Area vs. Super Built-Up

Always compare usable carpet area so you evaluate true living space accurately across projects.

RERA & Title Verification

Verify RERA registration status, approvals, and clear title documentation before committing a booking amount.

Daily Commute & Essentials

Test peak-hour travel times to work, schools, healthcare, and public transport during your site visit.
